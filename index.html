🌐 How Online Sharing & GitHub Hosting Works

We designed the online version to be lightweight (under 15 MB) so collaborators can open it on mobile phones without data or lag.

Collaborative Workflow:

  1. Online View: Collaborators open the GitHub Pages link on their phones or laptops.
  2. Make Edits: They review questions, click "✏️ Edit", and change any wrong answer letters, text, or notes.
  3. Export Edits: When done, they click the "📥 Export Edits" button in the top navbar. It downloads a lightweight forensic_edits_*.json file.
  4. Send to You: They send you this JSON file (via WhatsApp, Telegram, or Email).
  5. One-Click Import: You open your local visualizer, click "📤 Import Edits", select their file, and it automatically updates your dataset after you verify!
